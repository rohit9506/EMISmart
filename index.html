Part 1 · Fundamentals

What exactly is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed monthly payment that repays both principal and interest over a chosen term.

The Two Components

Every EMI payment combines two distinct portions

P

Principal Portion

Directly reduces the original amount borrowed from the bank.

I

Interest Portion

Pays the lender for borrowing capital based on your rate.

Early EMIs are interest-heavy, while late EMIs are principal-heavy.

Part 2 · Formula Logic

How reducing-balance EMI is calculated

Standard Reducing Balance Formula used by major banks globally.

1. Monthly Rate (R)

Annual interest rate divided by 12 months:
R = Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100

2. Total Months (N)

Tenure in years converted into total installments:
N = Tenure in Years × 12

3. Reducing Balance

Interest is calculated only on the remaining unpaid principal at the start of each month.

Mathematical Formula:
EMI = P × R × (1+R)N / [(1+R)N − 1]

Where P = Principal (up to ₹10 Cr), R = Monthly Rate, and N = Tenure Months.

Part 3 · Key Variables

Three levers that control your EMI

Every EMI payment is determined by three controllable parameters.

Loan Principal (Up to ₹10 Cr)

The total capital borrowed. Increasing principal increases EMI proportionally. A higher down payment reduces required principal.

Direct Scaling

Tenure (N)

The loan term in years. Longer tenure lowers monthly EMI burden but increases total accumulated interest paid to the lender.

Trade-off Factor

Interest Rate (R)

The rate charged by the bank. Even a 0.5% rate reduction saves lakhs on long-term loans.

Part 7 · Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about EMI calculation, interest rates, and loan repayments.

Can this calculator handle large loans up to ₹10 Crore?
Yes. The calculator math engine is designed to scale up to ₹10 Crore (`₹10,00,00,000`) for commercial real estate, luxury housing, and corporate borrowing with full precision.
How do the live Amortization Schedule controls work?
The Amortization section includes dedicated input boxes for Loan Amount, Tenure, and Rate. Adjusting them immediately recalculates the year-by-year amortization schedule table and visual timeline below it.
Why does total interest increase when loan tenure is longer?
A longer tenure spreads principal repayment over more months, which reduces your monthly EMI amount. However, because interest is accrued every month on the outstanding principal, extending the tenure means paying interest for a longer time, resulting in significantly higher total interest paid.
How does prepayment affect my EMI or tenure?
Making partial prepayments directly reduces your outstanding loan principal. You can test your prepayments in Part 5 using the Prepayment Simulator to see exact tenure years saved.
Is this financial advice?
No. This web tool is an educational math simulator to help you plan and analyze loan choices. Always confirm exact interest rates, fees, and official amortization schedules directly with your chosen lending bank before signing agreement documents.
